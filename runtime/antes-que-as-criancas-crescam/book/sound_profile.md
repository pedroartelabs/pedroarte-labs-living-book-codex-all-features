# LIVING SOUND profile

Primary acoustic organism: the Ferraz home.

The house begins noisy and layered: voices, television, steps, water, cutlery, toys, doors. During the adults' obsession, mediated and anticipatory sounds intrude: phones, requests to repeat, waiting silences. Later, small material sounds recover value: fabric, rain, packaging, chair legs, laces. The ending is not absolute silence; the house has changed its acoustic language.

Generic sad piano is not a valid default.
